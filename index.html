How it works

Four steps, all on chain.

A signed message opens a campaign, a post on X links a clipper's wallet, and Solana keeps the books.

  1. 1

    Fund

    A creator signs a campaign (title, their pump.fun link, a rate per 1,000 views, how many days) and sends SOL to its own escrow address.

  2. 2

    Clip

    Clippers post short videos on X with the campaign's link and submit the post. Their X account is linked to a wallet once, by a post.

  3. 3

    Verify

    The keeper re-reads each post's public views, likes, replies and reposts, and holds anything that looks bought.

  4. 4

    Pay

    Every hour new views are paid from the escrow at the campaign's rate. When it ends, what is left goes back to the creator.

FAQ

Questions

Is this pump.fun?

No. CreatorPay is an independent project, not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by pump.fun. It is built for creators in pump.fun Creators, who earn from traders they refer, and pays the clippers who post their link.

Where do the view counts come from?

From X's public post data, the same numbers anyone sees under a post. The keeper re-reads each accepted clip every 10 minutes.

What stops bought views?

X accounts must be old enough, a clip needs a real video and the campaign link, one account can take only part of a campaign, and views without likes, replies or reposts are held.

Who holds the SOL?

Each campaign's SOL sits in its own escrow address, a wallet run by the keeper. Every movement is a public Solana transaction with a memo, so the whole ledger can be rebuilt from the chain. An on-chain escrow program comes next.

What happens to unspent SOL?

When a campaign ends, or its creator closes it, clips are paid for the views they have and everything left goes back to the creator's wallet.

When do I get paid?

Every 60 minutes, straight to the wallet your X account is linked to, at the campaign's rate per 1,000 views.

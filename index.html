Reach is the bottleneck.

pump.fun's Creator Program pays approved creators a lifetime share of the trading fees of every trader they refer, and a $100,000 competition runs November 2 to 16, ranked by new referred volume. One creator can only post so much. CreatorPay lets them buy reach the way brands already do: a crowd of clippers, paid per verified view.

Live campaigns

Each campaign has its own escrow address on Solana. Its balance, every payout and the refund of what is left are public transactions.

Top clips

View counts are re-read from X's public data every 10 minutes. Payouts settle every 60.

Payouts

Every payout is one Solana transaction from a campaign's escrow, with a memo naming the campaign and each clip with the views it pays for: CreatorPay <campaign> | <clip>:<views>.

How it works

No accounts, no forms to trust. A signed message opens a campaign, a post on X links a clipper's wallet, and the chain keeps the books.

01

Fund

A creator signs a campaign (title, their pump.fun link, a rate per 1,000 views, how many days) and sends SOL to its own escrow address.

02

Clip

Clippers post short videos on X that include the campaign's link, then submit the post. Their X account is linked once to a wallet by a post.

03

Verify

The keeper re-reads each post's public view count, likes, replies and reposts, and holds anything that looks bought.

04

Pay

Every hour, new views are paid from the escrow at the campaign's rate. When it ends, what is left goes back to the creator.
